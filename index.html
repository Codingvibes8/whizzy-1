FAQ

Common questions

Do I need to be good with technology?

No. I set everything up and show you how to add items, change prices and handle orders from your phone.

Do you take a cut of my sales?

Never. Payments go straight to your own Stripe account. You only pay Stripe's standard card fees.

Can you add features to my existing website?

Yes. I can embed a shop or menu on WordPress, Wix, Squarespace or Webflow without rebuilding your site.

Who owns the website?

You do: the domain, the code and your customer data. If you ever want to move on, you take everything with you.

How long does it take?

Most Starter sites launch in about two weeks; Business builds take three to four.
